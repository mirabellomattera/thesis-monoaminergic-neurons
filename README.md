# thesis-monoaminergic-neurons

# Development and molecular evolution of human monoaminergic neurons

Figures and scripts accompanying the master's thesis of Mirabello Mattera (Master's Degree in Biodiversity and Evolution, University of Bologna; supervisor: Prof. Roberto Feuda).

## Contents
- `figures/`: high-resolution versions of the figures presented in the thesis (RNA-seq differential expression and temporal analyses; positive selection, GO and KEGG analyses).
- `scripts/`: scripts used for quantification (Kallisto), differential expression analysis (DESeq2), visualization, and the positive selection pipeline (OrthoDB, MAFFT, PAL2NAL, aBSREL; in `scripts/positive_selection/`).

Underlying result tables and raw data are not included.
